# Shipped log — how we got here

Reverse-chronological record of what each cycle shipped, as built: what it does, PR
number, date, and any migration or infra step it required.

⛔ **Not session-start reading.** `implementation-status.md` is loaded every session and
answers *"is this already done?"*; this one answers *"why is it like this?"*. Why they
are two files → `WORKFLOW.md` §2.5.

Consult it by targeted search: "why is X the way it is", "which PR closed Y". It
**cedes** to `implementation-status.md`, the specs and `backlog.md`: entries here are
what a terminal believed at the time, including beliefs later corrected.
